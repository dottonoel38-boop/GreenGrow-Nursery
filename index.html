Dedicated to supplying high-quality, healthy, and true-to-type seedlings while empowering farmers and landowners with practical horticultural solutions.

Our Aims

To bridge the gap between farmers and reliable planting materials, boost fruit and crop productivity, and promote sustainable green spaces.

Our Functions

Propagating healthy nursery seedlings, guiding growers on proper planting and care, supplying inputs/services, and supporting commercial and home gardens.

Our Foundations

Built on horticultural science, strict seedling selection, soil and plant health care, honesty with growers, and long-term agricultural impact.

Huge Plans

Expanding mother-block propagation, scaling commercial fruit nurseries, introducing modern greenhouse and irrigation solutions, and training more growers.
